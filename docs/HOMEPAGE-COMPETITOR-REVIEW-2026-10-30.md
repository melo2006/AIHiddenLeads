# Homepage offer and competitor review

Checked October 30, 2026. Public vendor claims are not independent performance verification.

## Current public offers

- Goodcall Starter: $79/month, 100 unique customers; repeat calls from the same number count once per month. Extra customers $0.79 each. Source: https://www.goodcall.com/pricing
- Smith.ai AI Receptionist Pro: $150/month, 75 calls. A free 25-call tier also exists; do not describe $150 as the universal entry price. Source: https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist
- Ruby Call Ruby 50: $250/month, 50 human receptionist minutes. Website chat is a separate service/bundle, not established as included in this phone plan. Source: https://rubyhelpcenter.helpjuice.com/en_US/billing-plans/am-i-on-the-right-billing-plan
- D-ID supports embedded website video, voice and chat: https://docs.d-id.com/docs/embed-overview
- Tavus developer plans include conversational video: https://www.tavus.io/pricing. Developer infrastructure is not an equivalent managed receptionist package.

## Positioning

Lead with business outcomes as goals: engaging visitors before they leave, answering routine questions, capturing inquiries, finding B2B prospects and helping teams focus on follow-up. Do not guarantee revenue, profit, ranking, universal savings or a two-minute scan. Emphasize the free actual-site simulation without installation and invite questions to Aspen.

Premium remains $99/month with 400 browser voice minutes and 50 B2B prospects during the current launch promotion; optional video is +$79/month for 400 video minutes. Professional and Platinum pricing is unchanged. Prospect allowances are offers, not proof of buyer interest or fulfilled outcomes.

Customer incoming telephone answering, forwarding, spend caps and transfers await the chosen external runtime and real end-to-end verification. Customer calendar connections and automatic CRM/report delivery are not established by the owner's platform calendar, private admin CSV/report tools or tested Hostinger operational emails. SMS and autonomous social publishing must not be described as live.

## Presentation decisions

- Keep the existing page order, site imagery, Aspen identity and commercial values.
- Tighten homepage-only section bands and remove the duplicate navbar-height gap.
- Use transparent portrait interaction surfaces, while retaining keyboard focus indication.
- Gemini remains the audible voice; suppress the Simli remote audio track independently of its SDK-controlled mute state.
- Label illustrative business scenarios as examples, not customer testimonials or quantified client results.

## Suggested next step

Replace illustrative examples with permissioned customer evidence when available, and measure demo-to-inquiry outcomes before making performance claims. Do not add more competing hero buttons.
