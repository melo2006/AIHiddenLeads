# Telephone-first personalized demos

Public sources inspected October 31, 2026; vendor descriptions are not independent product tests. Prices can change and billing units differ.

## Comparable offers
- AIR describes a website scan and a demo telephone number: https://demo.aireceptionist.work/
- Bookbag describes a website/phone input followed by calling a demo line: https://app.bookbag.ai/try
- Wolf Bot offers browser or callback demos: https://wolfbot.ai/demo
- Ciela describes business-personalized shared demos and consented calls, not necessarily inbound spoken-URL setup: https://ciela.ai/agents/ai-receptionist
- Engine & Agent advertises a callable AI receptionist demo, phone and web chat, starting $99/month for 100 phone minutes with $249 setup: https://engineandagent.com/services/ai-receptionist/
- Goodcall lists $79/$129/$299 tiers based on unique customers, not minutes: https://www.goodcall.com/pricing
- Trillet lists $49/month for 150 minutes then $0.20/min, and separate agency plans: https://trillet.ai/pricing
- Smith.ai uses per-call pricing, not directly comparable minute bundles: https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist
- Retell is metered voice infrastructure rather than a fully configured small-business package: https://www.retellai.com/pricing

Exact all-spoken inbound flow (caller spells URL during existing call, immediate roleplay in the same call) was not independently verified. These sources establish related personalized telephone demos, not demand size, conversion improvement or a unique market opening.

## Recommendation, not an approved offer
Use a free, bounded telephone demonstration as another entry into the same plans. Keep $99 Premium / $199 Professional / $349 Platinum; make website widget optional for telephone customers instead of proliferating tariffs. Keep website allowances distinct from the intended $0.05 telephone minute rate, which needs verified total telephony/AI/hosting/transfer economics before billing promises. Do not introduce unlimited calls or a lower-priced phone-only plan without usage evidence.

Telephone flow: ask permission; spell/read back website and business name; validate and safely learn the confirmed public URL; explain partial learning; announce fictional-business roleplay; conduct demo; explicitly return to AI Hidden Leads sales; request contact/follow-up only with consent. Never book or transfer for the simulated business. If learning is slow, offer a generic clearly labelled simulation or an opted-in demo link/callback, not a fabricated instant scan. Spoken URLs and protected websites are likely friction points.

Merit: busy/mobile owners can hear the experience without switching to a screen. Risk: slow scans, speech-recognition URL errors, paid demo abuse, and confusion between simulation and live deployment. Validate with a small owner-reviewed pilot measuring completion, failures, qualified follow-ups and cost; competitors do not establish proven demand.

Natural Gemini Live telephone runtime is not deployed. No phone routing, greeting, customer offer or public pricing changed as part of this report research.
